(How it works)

Four steps from hazard to highway.

No account needed to file. Every report is geotagged, time-stamped and permanently traceable.

01

Snap & pinpoint

Take a photo and we auto-fill GPS coordinates, barangay and nearest landmark.

02

Classify severity

Pick the hazard type and severity so dispatch can rank it against other open items.

03

Verify on site

A field officer confirms the report with a photo before work orders are issued.

04

Fixed & closed

You get a notification and can rate the resolution once the crew finishes.

(Impact)

Built with barangays, not just for them.

RoadWatch was designed alongside municipal disaster officers, barangay captains and volunteer validators. The goal was simple: one place where a resident's photo turns into a verified work order with an owner and a deadline.

  • Full audit trailEvery state change is logged with the officer, timestamp and reason.
  • Dual verificationReports need a photo check before they reach the public work queue.
  • Offline-first filingQueued submissions sync automatically when signal returns.
  • Open data exportAny LGU can pull an anonymised dataset for budgeting and planning.
